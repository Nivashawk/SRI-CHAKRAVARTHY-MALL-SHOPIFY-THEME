Dispatch

Orders are dispatched within two working days.

Delivery within India

Delivery takes three to seven days depending on the pin code. You will get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Shipping is free on orders over ₹4,999.

International delivery

We ship to most countries. International orders over ₹39,999 ship free.

Customs duties, import taxes and any local charges are payable by the recipient. These are set by the destination country and are not within our control, so we cannot tell you the amount in advance.

If something goes wrong

If your tracking link has not moved for several days, or the parcel arrives damaged, contact us before opening any dispute with the carrier and we will chase it for you.
